Chapter one

Our story

Eight years, three cities and one very persistent group chat. Here's the short version.

  1. how-we-met.jpg

    March 2018 · Pune

    How we met

    A mutual friend's birthday dinner in Koregaon Park. Shivam spent twenty minutes explaining warehouse software to someone who had not asked. Ayushi listened anyway, then told him his slides needed work. He has been taking her notes ever since.

    She left first. He asked the birthday girl for her number the next morning, which took four days of negotiation and one promise to never again mention inventory cycle counts on a first meeting.

  2. first-date.jpg

    April 2018 · Sinhagad Fort

    The first date

    He suggested a sunrise trek. She said yes, then discovered he meant a 4:30 am start. They made it to the top for pithla bhakri and kanda bhaji, and stayed talking until the fog burned off.

    Neither of them mentions that the descent took three hours because someone's shoes gave up. Eight years on, the same shoes are still in the cupboard, unworn and unforgiven.

  3. the-proposal.jpg

    December 2025 · London

    The proposal

    A work trip that turned into a long weekend. On a cold Sunday by the Thames, with the ring in a coat pocket for eleven hours, Shivam finally stopped rehearsing and just asked. Ayushi said yes before he finished the sentence.

    The photo was taken by a stranger who insisted on twelve retakes. We owe him everything, and we never got his name.

What to wear

A dress code guide

No rules you have to follow, only colours we'd love to see. December evenings in Ujjain drop to about 8–10°C, so bring a shawl or jacket for the lawn.

Mehendi & Haldi
Cottons and bright florals. Sleeves you can push up.
Sangeet

Ceremony
Sage, ivory and gold. Sarees, lehengas, bandhgalas, suits.

One ask: please avoid red and full white for the ceremony.

Getting here

Travel

Indore's Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport (IDR) is about 1.5 hours from the venue and the nearest major airport. Ujjain Junction railway station is well connected to Delhi, Mumbai, and Indore, and about 20 minutes from Parmeshwari Garden & Resort.

While you're here

Make a few days of it

  • 01

    Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga Temple

    One of the twelve jyotirlingas. The Bhasma Aarti before sunrise is worth the early start.

  • 02

    Ram Ghat, Shipra River

    Best at dusk for the evening aarti, with boats along the ghats.

  • 03

    Kal Bhairav Temple

    A short drive from the venue, known for its distinctive offering tradition.

  • 04

    Vedh Shala (Jantar Mantar)

    A centuries-old observatory, quiet and easy to combine with the temples nearby.

  • 05

    Sandipani Ashram

    A peaceful stop linked to Krishna's education, on the edge of the old city.
