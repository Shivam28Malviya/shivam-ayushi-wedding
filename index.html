    What to wear

    A dress code guide

    No rules you have to follow, only colours we'd love to see. December evenings in Ujjain drop to about 8–10°C, so bring a shawl or jacket for the lawn.

    One ask: please avoid red and full white for the ceremony.

    Getting here

    Travel

    Indore's Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport (IDR) is about 1.5 hours from the venue and the nearest major airport. Ujjain Junction is well connected to Delhi, Mumbai and Indore, and about 20 minutes away.
